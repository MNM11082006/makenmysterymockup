Principal's Welcome

Welcome to Our School Family

Welcome to Dr. Macken Mistry Primary School. Located at the heart of the Sea Cow Lake community, our dedicated educators work tirelessly to cultivate a safe, inclusive, and academically vibrant environment for every child.

We focus on holistic development—combining foundational curriculum strengths with strong ethical values, sports, and community engagement. Through invaluable partnerships with organizations like Gift of the Givers, we continuously improve our infrastructure and learner support. We invite parents and partners to join us in shaping the leaders of tomorrow.

Enrollment Process

3 Simple Steps to Register Your Child

Follow these straightforward steps to secure your child’s placement for the upcoming academic year.

1

Prepare Documents

  • Child's Birth Certificate
  • Parent / Guardian ID Document
  • Latest School Report Card
  • Clinic Card / Immunization Record
  • Proof of Residence
2

Collect or Download Form

Obtain an official registration form directly from the school administration office or request a copy digitally via WhatsApp.

3

Submit at School Admin

Visit our admissions desk at 87 Macken Mistry Road during operating hours (Mon–Fri, 07:30–14:30) to submit forms and complete registration.

Need help with registration?

Contact our admin office directly via WhatsApp or phone call for support.
